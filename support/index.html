Questions

How do I back up my data?

In the app, open Settings → Export and backup → Back up all data. Save the file in Files or send it to yourself. To bring it back, use Restore from backup… on the same screen.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

With iCloud on for the app, sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and install the app. Your data syncs. Without iCloud, make a backup on the old iPhone and restore it on the new one.

The app read a number wrong from my dashboard photo.

Tap the value and correct it before you save. If you keep dashboard photos (Settings → Keep dashboard photos), later versions of the app can read them again.

My state or electricity company is missing, or the rate is wrong.

Open Settings → Tariff plans to copy a plan and change it, or to make your own rate. Please also email me the tariff order or a bill, so I can fix it for everyone.
