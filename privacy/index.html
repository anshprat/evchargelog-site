EV Charge Log

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Summary. EV Charge Log does not collect your data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer has no server and cannot see anything you put in the app. Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage.

This policy is for the EV Charge Log app for iPhone and its widget and Shortcuts (together, "the app"). The app is made by Anshu Prateek, an individual developer ("I", "me").

1. The data the app keeps

The app keeps only the data you enter or create in it:

  • Vehicles: make, model, variant and, if you add it, the registration number.
  • Charging sessions: dates and times, dashboard readings (battery %, range, odometer, trip values), energy, cost and notes.
  • Photos: the dashboard and receipt photos you take or choose, and the text the app reads from them.
  • Places: the charging places you save, with the name, address and map position.
  • Electricity: your tariff plan, connection details and the bills you enter.
  • Settings: your choices in the app, for example the active vehicle.

2. Where the data is stored

  • On your iPhone. All data is stored on the device.
  • In your iCloud, if you use it. When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for the app, the app syncs your data and photos to your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple Account can read it. I cannot read it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

The app does not send your data to me or to any other company.

3. Camera and photos

The app uses the camera and your photo library only when you take or choose a dashboard or receipt photo. The app reads the text in the photo on your iPhone, with Apple's Vision framework. The photo does not leave your iPhone, except to your own iCloud as described above. You can turn off Settings → Keep dashboard photos, and you can delete photos in Settings → Photos.

4. Location

  • While you use the app: the app uses your location to suggest the charging place for a session.
  • Always (optional, off by default): only if you turn on Warn when leaving without the after photo. The app then asks iOS to tell it when you leave a saved charging place while a session is still open.

Location stays on your iPhone. Saved places are stored with your other data as described above.

5. Files you export

The app makes backup (JSON) and CSV files only when you ask for them. You choose where each file goes. After that, the app you send it to, or the place you save it, controls the file.

6. Purchases

If the app offers paid features, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. I do not get your payment details. The app checks your purchases with Apple on your iPhone.

7. Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data: no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. The app does not share data with third parties and does not track you across apps or websites.

8. Children

The app is not for children under 13. Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

9. Deleting your data

  • Delete a session, vehicle, place, bill or photo in the app.
  • Delete the app to remove all data from your iPhone.
  • To delete the iCloud copy, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → EV Charge Log on your iPhone, then delete the data.

Because I hold none of your data, I cannot delete it for you, and I cannot restore it if you delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will publish the new version on this page and change the date at the top.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, email evchargelog@e3l.in.
