EV Charge Log

Log every home charge of your electric car or scooter. Take a photo of the dashboard before and after. The app reads the numbers and works out the units and the cost on your electricity tariff.

For iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

  • Reads battery %, range and odometer from dashboard photos, on your iPhone.
  • Electricity tariffs for every state and union territory in India, with free-unit subsidies.
  • Cost per charge, ₹/km and kWh/100 km, with trends over months.
  • Cars and two-wheelers. Home Screen widget and Shortcuts.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
